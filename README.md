# 🪷 Hidden India | Discover India's Secret Treasures & Sacred Landscapes

> **"India has stories you've never heard."**  
> A frontend hackathon web application dedicated to discovering India's lesser-known destinations, forgotten historical places, local traditions, hidden natural wonders, sacred sites, waterfalls, and cultural stories.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🪷 The Discover India Homepage (Page 1)
- **Full-Screen Cinematic Hero**: Featuring the headline *"India has stories you've never heard"*, interactive quick search, live subcontinent statistics ticker, and curated tags.
- **Ambient Indian Soundscape**: Built using the Web Audio API with soothing Tanpura harmonics (432Hz) and gentle Tibetan singing bowl / temple bell chimes that can be toggled on/off.
- **Hidden Gem of the Day**: Spotlight card showcasing daily featured offbeat sanctuaries.
- **Category & Region Filtering**: Filter by Sacred & Mythological, Waterfalls & Valleys, Mountain Mystics, Tribal & Village Life, Ancient Stone & Heritage, and Mysterious Landscapes across North & Northeast, South, and East & West.
- **"Why Hidden India?" Mission**: Highlighting the problem of commercial overtourism and our solution to preserve cultural heritage.

### 2. 📖 Stories Beyond Tourism (Page 2)
Deep-dive interactive story modals for every destination, featuring:
- **History & Forgotten Chronicles**: Ancient excavations and archaeological discoveries.
- **Local Legends & Sacred Folklore**: Tales of sages, celestial beings, and divine lore (e.g. Shiva's curse at Unakoti, the Donyi-Polo spirits of Ziro, Rama & Jatayu at Lepakshi).
- **Living Culture & Artisans**: Apatani facial tattoos, Majuli mask-makers at Natun Samaguri Satra, Athangudi handmade cement tile casting, living root bridge training.
- **Lost Recipes & Cuisine**: Regional culinary heritage (Assamese Khar, Chettinad peppercorn curries, bamboo shoot delicacies, Kutchi dabeli).
- **How to Reach Respectfully**: Ethical explorer guidance and transit recommendations.
- **High-Definition Photography Gallery**: Multi-image view for each sanctuary.

### 3. 🗺️ Interactive Map of India (Page 3)
- Interactive stylized SVG map of the Indian subcontinent with clickable regional pins.
- Filter pins by region (Northeast, South, North, West, Central).
- Instant state preview drawer displaying hidden highlights, cultural badges, and direct links to full stories.
- Toggle between Interactive Map view and State Directory grid.

### 4. 🧭 "Choose Your Secret Trail" (Page 4 - The Flagship Hackathon Feature)
A custom interactive recommendation engine asking what kind of India visitors want to discover:
1. **Choose your interests**:
   - Ancient mysteries and forgotten history
   - Mythology and sacred places
   - Nature and hidden villages
   - Local culture, crafts and traditions
2. **How long is your trip?**:
   - One day
   - 2–3 days
   - One week
3. **Preferred region**:
   - Anywhere in India
   - South India
   - North and Northeast India
   - East and West India
- **Confetti Celebration**: Vibrant celebratory burst using `canvas-confetti`.
- **Custom Day-by-Day Secret Itinerary**: Morning, midday, and evening breakdown, recommended season, and insider packing tips.
- **Save Trail**: Directly save the generated itinerary into your personal Journey Passport.

### 5. 🧳 My Journey & Visited Passport (Page 5)
- **Explorer Passport Progress Gauge**: Level gauge from *Novice Wanderer 🌿* to *Master of Hidden India 🪷* based on the percentage of secret gems discovered.
- **Places I Have Visited**: Stamped checklist of visited places.
- **Traveler Opinions & Reviews**: Log visits, provide 1–5 star ratings, write personal reflections, and share insider tips for future travelers.
- **Saved Wishlist**: Personal bucket list of destinations to explore.
- **Local Storage Persistence**: All progress, reviews, wishlist items, and saved trails persist automatically in your browser.

### 6. 👤 Login & Profile Section
- **1-Click Instant Demo Login**: Hackathon-ready test profiles (*Rohan Sharma*, *Priya Sengupta*, *Ananya Iyer*) for instant demonstration.
- **Custom Sign Up / Log In**: Create a custom explorer account with name, email, password, and custom avatar emblem (🪷, 🧭, ✨, ⛰️, 🦅).
- **Profile Stats**: Live counters for visited places and wishlist treasures.

### 7. 📱 100% Responsive for Mobile & Desktop
- **Laptop / Desktop**: Multi-column layouts, cinematic widescreen heroes, floating modals, and interactive SVG map.
- **Mobile Phones & Tablets**: Touch-friendly buttons (min 44px hit targets), horizontal scroll chips, hamburger drawer, and persistent bottom navigation bar.

## 🏛️ Featured Destinations

1. **Ziro Valley, Arunachal Pradesh** — Nature, Indigenous Apatani culture, pine groves, paddy-fish farming.
2. **Unakoti, Tripura** — Ancient rock carvings of Lord Shiva, mysterious 9,999,999 stone faces.
3. **Majuli, Assam** — World's largest river island, 500-year-old Satras, bamboo mask-making, pottery.
4. **Chettinad, Tamil Nadu** — Palatial 100-room Athangudi tile mansions, spicy Chettinad cuisine, antique crafts.
5. **Gandikota, Andhra Pradesh** — The "Grand Canyon of India" carved by the Pennar River in red granite.
6. **Phugtal Monastery, Ladakh** — Cliffside cave gompa suspended over a turquoise river gorge.
7. **Mawlynnong & Living Root Bridges, Meghalaya** — Bio-engineered Ficus elastica suspension bridges and Asia's cleanest village.
8. **Lonar Meteorite Lake, Maharashtra** — 52,000-year-old hypervelocity meteorite crater lake in basalt plains.
9. **Lepakshi Veerabhadra Temple, Andhra Pradesh** — Defying-gravity hanging pillar, monolithic Nandi, Vijayanagara murals.
10. **Chopta & Tungnath, Uttarakhand** — World's highest Shiva shrine at 3,680m amidst rhododendron meadows.
11. **Athirappilly & Vazhachal, Kerala** — The "Niagara of South India" cascading 80 feet through lush rainforests.
12. **Dholavira, Gujarat** — 5,000-year-old Harappan white salt desert metropolis with monumental reservoirs.

## 🚀 Running the Project Locally

```bash
# 1. Install dependencies
npm install

# 2. Start the development server
npm run dev

# 3. Open in browser:
# http://localhost:3000
```

To create a production build:
```bash
npm run build
```
